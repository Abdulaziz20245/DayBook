How the art is made
  1. Seed = FNV-1a hash of date + entry text, fed to mulberry32. Same entry, same art, every time.
  2. Mood = best keyword-prefix match over the six lists (no match: picked by seed from the five lighter moods).
  3. Background: two soft radial glows (palette 1 and 2) over a linear base from palette 4 to 3, random angle.
  4. Shapes: 7 on the full card, 3 on thumbnails, none on mini mosaics. Circle, ring, half-disc, pill, blurred blob; 35% use multiply blend.
  5. Shapes are % positioned in a square, so one recipe renders at 36 px or 1080 px. Export the card with canvas or html-to-image.
